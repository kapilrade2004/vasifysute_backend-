# 1. Platform Overview & Core Principles

Vasify SUITE is a **modular business management SaaS platform** where organizations can select the services they need and operate them from one unified workspace.

### Core Principles
The platform natively supports:
- Multiple organizations with strict isolation.
- Multiple users per organization with granular role permissions.
- Pluggable business services (CRM, Finance, HR, Projects, Workspace, and more).
- Service-based subscriptions and automated activation/deactivation.
- Organization-specific customizations (custom fields, pipelines, layouts, workflows, branding).
- Cross-service consolidated reporting and analytics.
- Centralized multi-channel notifications (In-app, Email, WhatsApp, Push).
- Centralized document repository and digital asset vault.
- Complete chronological audit trails and activity timelines.
- Drag-and-drop business automations and approval hierarchies.
- External client portals and internal employee self-service portals.
- Comprehensive Master Platform Administration.

# 3. Account Registration & Onboarding

## Registration
- Multi-step sign up: Name, work email, phone number, password.
- Verification flow: Instant email verification and mobile OTP.
- Organization Setup: Company legal name, subdomain/slug, country, currency, timezone, tax GSTIN, and company logo upload.

## Service Selection
- Tenant checks which modules to activate: `[✓] CRM`, `[✓] Finance`, `[✓] HR`, `[✓] Projects`, `[✓] Workspace`.

## Plan Selection
- Choose tier: Starter, Professional, or Enterprise.
- Configure initial user licenses, add-on storage, apply coupon, and activate trial or subscribe.

## Guided Onboarding Wizard
1. Company Profile Confirmation: Working hours, fiscal year, default currency.
2. Industry Selection: Presets loaded for Agency, Real Estate, Education, Consulting, or Healthcare.
3. Team Invitations: Bulk email invite for staff with role assignment.
4. Data Import: One-click upload for CSV/Excel customer contacts or past invoices.
5. Initial Dashboard: Tailored widgets launched immediately upon completion.

## CRM Analytics & Reports

# 8. Finance & Accounting

## Finance Dashboard
- Total Revenue, Operating Expenses, Net Margin, Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable, Cash Flow trends, and GST Tax summary.

## Quotations & Sales Orders
- Create formal quotations with line items, tax breakdowns, terms, and discounts.
- Send via Email or WhatsApp with public acceptance links.
- 1-Click conversion: Quotation ➔ Sales Order ➔ Tax Invoice.

## Invoices & Recurring Billing
- Create GST tax invoices with automatic intra-state (CGST + SGST) vs inter-state (IGST) tax calculation.
- Support for multi-bracket tax rates: `0% (Exempt)`, `5%`, `12%`, `18%`, `28%`.
- Auto-generation of serial invoice numbers (`INV-YYYYMM-XXXX`).
- Status lifecycle: `Draft`, `Sent`, `Paid`, `Partially Paid`, `Overdue`, `Cancelled`, `Void`.
- Automated recurring invoice generator with automatic email dispatch.
- Clean printable PDF invoice with company logo, bank account details, and UPI QR code.

## Payments & Receipts
- Record full or partial payments across cash, bank transfer, UPI, credit card, and cheques.
- Automatic balance calculation, advance payment allocation, and receipt generation.

## Expenses & Vendor Management
- Log operating expenses by category (`Travel`, `Software`, `Office`, `Marketing`).
- Vendor database, purchase orders, purchase bills, and vendor payment tracking.

## Credit & Debit Notes
- Issue credit notes for invoice adjustments or customer refunds with settlement ledgers.

## Financial Reports
- Sales ledgers, expense reports, accounts receivable aging, GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B tax export reports, and P&L summaries.
